About

Meridian is a small coffee roaster in Berlin. We started in 2019 with one machine and a short list of farms we believed in. Not much has changed since.

We buy green coffee directly or through importers we trust, and we roast it lightly — enough to develop sweetness, not so much that the origin disappears. Everything is roasted in batches small enough to taste as we go.

We keep the menu deliberately short. A handful of single origins, one everyday blend, rotated through the seasons. We would rather do a few coffees well than carry forty we can't keep fresh.

Coffee is shipped within days of roasting. If you ever get a bag that isn't right, write to us and we'll make it right.
